Intelligence & Battle Stats ▼
Estimated stats are now provided dynamically via FF Scouter. These estimates pull from public data and are refreshed hourly. Please understand that they are guidelines rather than absolute truth.

Sorting System: Members listed as "Okay" jump to the top. Hospitalized players follow, and traveling members sit at the bottom.

Correction System: Click any yellow stat badge to manually override the FF Scouter estimate with the real, verified numbers.

Backup System: Click the "SOS" button to flag an active enemy if you need a faction member to help you take them down.
